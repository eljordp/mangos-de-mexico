# Image requests for the mango site (for Codex)

The site at `mango-site/index.html` currently uses a drawn SVG mango as a placeholder. Please produce these and save them to `mango-site/images/`. Real photos are preferred over AI-generated ones, since these will be used to sell actual fruit.

| File | What | Size / format | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | A pile or crate of ripe Mexican mangos (Ataulfo + Kent mix), warm daylight, bright and appetizing, with some leaves if possible | 1600×1200, JPG, under 300 KB | Replaces the SVG mango in the hero (`.sign`) |
| `ataulfo.jpg` | One or two Ataulfo (honey) mangos, one cut open to show the flesh | 600×600, square | Varieties section |
| `kent.jpg` | Kent mango, whole plus a cheek scored "hedgehog" style | 600×600, square | Varieties section |
| `tommy.jpg` | Tommy Atkins mango showing the red/green skin | 600×600, square | Varieties section |
| `og-share.jpg` | Social preview: mangos plus the text "12 for $25 · Napa Mon · Sac Wed" | 1200×630 | `<meta property="og:image">` |

Notes:
- Keep the colors warm but not over-saturated, and don't clip the highlights on the skin.
- No logos or brand stickers on the fruit.
- The site's palette is mango orange `#FFA41B`, deep green `#173D27`, and chile red `#C8341C`.
